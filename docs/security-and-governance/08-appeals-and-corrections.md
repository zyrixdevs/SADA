# 08 — Appeals and corrections

Accept verified case-linked appeal, acknowledge without unapproved timeline, route to authorized reviewer independent where possible. Outcomes: uphold, correct, reverse, remand, withdraw. Whether appeal pauses future high-impact action is open; conservative proposal pauses pending triage. Corrections append linked revision, recompute, cancel stale delivery and notify authorized affected parties. Local actions remain local decisions.
## Appeal review record

Store appeal scope, receipt time, assigned reviewer, conflict check, requested correction, evidence reviewed, decision rationale, notification result, and next available step. Do not reveal reporter identities or unrelated server data to an appellant. A successful correction invalidates pending decision signals and schedules scoped notices to affected recipients.
