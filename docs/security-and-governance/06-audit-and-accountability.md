# 06 — Audit and accountability

Record actor, action, object/revision, request/correlation, UTC time, policy, outcome, reason code, minimal before/after. Include consent, evidence reads/exports, votes, reversal, credentials, holds and local receipts. No secrets/private content. App role cannot update/delete audit; correction appends linked event. Restrict audit reads; tamper evidence and retention open.
## Accountability review

A reviewer can see the policy/evidence revision and their own authority basis before voting. A periodic audit samples approvals, reversals, evidence exports, emergency overrides, and access denials. Findings are assigned to an owner with correction due date. Audit access itself is audited, with elevated review when an operator reads restricted evidence.
