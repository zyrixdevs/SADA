# 03 — Threat severity standard

Severity is response priority, not confidence/guilt. Proposed finite levels require approved urgency, recipients, review and expiry rules. High severity with low confidence remains uncertain. No severity authorizes punishment. Unknown is unclassified.
## Mapping and escalation

A severity policy maps a named threat class and potential impact to triage urgency, recipient roles, expiry, and required human review. It must not map directly to a Discord action. A change in severity can update advisory priority but cannot elevate confidence. Reviewers can downgrade or annotate a severity with rationale; the original automated proposal remains auditable.
