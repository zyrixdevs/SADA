# 04 — Confidence standard

Every assessment names proposition, evidence IDs, exclusions, independence, policy/algorithm version, uncertainty and review state. A bare score is not probability without calibration. Begin with factor-level or uncalibrated prioritization. Missing data is unknown; contradiction visible. Confidence never grants enforcement authority.

## Versioned factor snapshot

Store factor identifiers, values or qualitative states, evidence references, missingness, and weighting-method version. Record excluded evidence with a machine-readable reason such as duplicate, reversed, outside-window, or unrelated category. Recompute creates a new snapshot rather than updating old values. Reviewer overrides are separate judgments and never rewrite the model output.
