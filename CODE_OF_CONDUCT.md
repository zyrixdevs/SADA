# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. It applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and people acting as SADA representatives.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and applying these standards fairly. They may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code and should communicate the reason when appropriate. No moderation team or independent enforcement board is established by this document.

## Scope

This Code applies within SADA community spaces and when someone is officially representing the project in public. It does not replace GitHub's platform policies.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner or a maintainer through an established private GitHub contact route. Do not post private information or retaliation publicly. No dedicated Code of Conduct email address or response team is currently published. Maintainers should review reports promptly when capacity allows, protect reporter privacy, and avoid having a person who is the subject of a report act as its sole reviewer where another appropriate maintainer is available.

Maintainers may use proportionate responses such as a private correction, warning, temporary restriction from project spaces, or permanent restriction for repeated or severe conduct. Decisions should explain the basis when it is safe to do so. A contributor may ask the repository owner to reconsider a decision; no independent appeals panel exists today.
